## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-07

### Added
- Minimalist OLED Black & White Jetpack Compose user interface.
- Apple-style custom wheel picker for minutes and seconds timer setting.
- 0ms Offline-First Room SQLite Database persistence.
- 2-Way Background Delta Sync Engine with `X-Idempotency-Key` headers for duplicate prevention.
- Per-user account isolation with encrypted local credential caching (SHA-256).
- Standalone Inbox Tasks and Project management structure.
- Live unrounded remaining time display (`Rem: Xm Ys`) for active/paused sessions.
- Do Not Disturb mode integration and system settings targeting.
